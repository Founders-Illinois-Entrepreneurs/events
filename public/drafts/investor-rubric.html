The 1–5 scale

Writing useful feedback

  • Be specific. Point to a moment in the pitch, not a general feeling.
  • Name the one thing that would make you invest more. That is the team's homework before the final pitch.
  • Reward honesty. A team that says what it doesn't know yet is easier to back than one that hides it.
  • Back the team, not your friends. Your reasons are shared with the R&D teams.
